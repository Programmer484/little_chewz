# A Welcome from Mrs. Tongsley

Creative Director of Confetti Cake Cottage School

Welcome to Confetti Cake Cottage School! I'm Mrs. Tongsley, the Creative Director, and I have one of the sweetest jobs in the whole school. I get to help students discover that the best celebrations aren't about being perfect. They're about creating something meaningful together.

You might think Confetti Cake is all about cupcakes, frosting, and sprinkles. Those things are certainly part of the fun.

But the real magic happens when people share ideas, encourage one another, and keep going even when things don't turn out the way they planned.

As you journey through the Cake & Chaos Club, you'll investigate friendship mysteries, complete creative challenges, and discover that every setback is really an opportunity to learn, grow, and try again. Some days you'll be baking. Some days you'll be creating. Some days you'll be solving problems together. And some days you'll discover what you're capable of when you don't give up.

Remember, every masterpiece begins as a messy idea. So be brave enough to try, kind enough to encourage others, and proud of the effort you put into every challenge. I'll be cheering you on as you complete each of the quests and prepare for the Sparkle Festival.

The best celebrations come after the hardest work.

See you around the school,

Mrs. Tongsley  
Creative Director  
Confetti Cake Cottage School
