# Confetti File CCC-1958-01

## Bakery Log: October 18, 1958

Busy Saturday. Sold out of cinnamon buns before noon again. Mrs. Holloway stopped by after church. She mentioned that everyone seems to be baking from those new cake mixes these days and wondered if I'd ever sell one here. I told her if folks wanted cake from a box, they could buy one at the grocer. Around here, we still believe butter belongs in a mixing bowl, not a factory. She laughed. I think she agreed.

Finished decorating three birthday cakes before lunch. Little Tommy Dawson was so excited to carry his birthday cake home that he nearly dropped it before reaching the door. His mother gave him a tug on the ear and reminded him that cakes don't bounce.

Something odd happened again this morning. That little cupcake was on the cookbook shelf. I know for certain I left her on the windowsill before locking up last night. Mary says someone must be playing tricks. Maybe she's right. Still...I've never actually seen anyone move her.

We've all started calling her Callie now. Strange little thing. Seems she's part of the bakery now. Best head home. The bread won't bake itself tomorrow.

— M.T.
